# Archive Fashion Index

A **highly curated** digital archive and index of past, current, and emerging **high fashion, archival, avant-garde, and niche** fashion brands — with a strong emphasis on **Asian fashion** (Japan, South Korea, China, and beyond), while also featuring notable European and American designers for broader context.

This repository functions as a **living reference** and discovery platform for designers, researchers, collectors, stylists, fashion students, and enthusiasts who want to explore the depth of fashion history and contemporary creativity.

## Project Goals

- Preserve and highlight often under-documented fashion brands, especially from Asia
- Create an organized, easy-to-navigate index of archival and high-fashion houses
- Maintain **high editorial quality** through careful curation
- Serve as both a research tool and a source of inspiration

## What’s Inside a Country Folder?

Each country folder is self-contained and includes:

- **`Country.md`** — A single, alphabetically organized master list of all documented brands from that country. - **`Styles/`** folder — Curated thematic lists (e.g., Avant-Garde, Punk, Minimal, Streetwear, Visual Kei, etc.) containing links to relevant brands from the main list.

This structure allows users to browse both **geographically** and **aesthetically**.

## Brand Entry Template

All brands follow a consistent format defined in [`Templates/brand-template.md`](brand-template.md). This ensures readability and high information quality across the archive.

Typical entries include:

- Origin, founding year, and founder 
- Detailed description and cultural significance
- Signature style and techniques 
- Notable collections or moments 
- Official links (website, Instagram, etc.) 
- Visual references (screenshots or lookbook images)

## Contributing

This is a **community-curated** project. Everyone is welcome to participate!

You can contribute by:

- Adding new brands - Expanding existing entries with better descriptions or links - Creating or improving style lists - Adding high-quality images/screenshots

### How to Contribute

1. **Recommended**: Fork the repository, make your changes, and submit a **Pull Request**. 
2. **Alternative**: Open a **Git Issue** with the brand name, relevant links, and any information you may have.

All submissions will be **carefully reviewed** before being merged. The goal is to maintain a high-quality, accurate, and well-curated archive — low-effort or poorly researched entries will not be accepted.

## Guidelines

- Prioritize archival value, cultural relevance, and creativity - Focus especially on Asian designers and niche/underground brands - Provide sources when possible - Use the official brand template for consistency
